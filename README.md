## Hi, I'm Shloka

I'm a high school student in Bengaluru who likes finding out whether things are actually true. Most of what's here started as a question I couldn't answer by looking it up, and turned into code because that was the only way to find out.

**[Topspin as an Aerodynamic Stabiliser](https://github.com/shloka04/table-tennis-simulation)** — Table tennis coaches say to loop rather than hit flat. I wanted to know what topspin really does once you accept that nobody executes a shot perfectly, so I modelled each shot as a distribution rather than a trajectory. It turns out topspin works as a stabiliser: it shrinks how far your mistakes move the ball, by 62%. The model predicts 133 real recorded shots it had never seen to a median error of 37.8 mm, with every physical parameter fixed in advance.
*Published at SIMULTECH 2026, Porto — [doi:10.5220/0015182700004094](https://doi.org/10.5220/0015182700004094)*

**[Air Quality and Respiratory Health in Indian Cities](https://github.com/shloka04/air_quality_respiratory_health)** — I expected India's dirtiest states to carry the heaviest respiratory disease burden. The correlation came out negative. Tracking down why — age structure and how thoroughly each state diagnoses illness — taught me more than a clean positive result would have. 703 city-years across 258 cities.

**[Crop Suitability & Recommendation](https://github.com/shloka04/crop-suitability-system)** — Two models predicting which crops suit a field's soil and weather, wrapped in a web app. One of them reports 100% accuracy, which is wrong, and the README explains exactly why.

**[Urban Noise and Mental Health](https://github.com/shloka04/urban_noise_mental_health)** — Do noisier New York neighbourhoods report more depression? Every 311 noise complaint against neighbourhood depression data, with a noise index weighted by time of day and severity.
*Published in Curieux Academic Journal*

**[PetFindrr](https://github.com/shloka04/stonehill_hack)** — A swipe-based pet adoption app built over a weekend at the Stonehill hackathon, with a recommendation model that learns from what you swipe on.
